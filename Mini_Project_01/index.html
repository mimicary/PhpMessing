Art With No Audience Why People Create


Vivian Maier

× 



        Why do people create?
        Whether it's a small project like a doodle to a lifelong dedication to one project, why does it happen?
        What compels us as humans to create, destroy and edit art that won't be seen by anyone, sometimes not even themselves?
        When in the end it shall disappear anyways, consumed by time and forgotten by all.
